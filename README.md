# 🔬 Applied Mathematics, Physics & Computational Engineering Portfolio

An independent summer research portfolio integrating university-level mathematics, physics simulations, and electrical engineering models in Python.

---

## 📌 Project Overview & Learning Roadmap

---

## 1. Mathematical Foundations & Data Modeling

### 📐 Vector Calculus
* **Core Concepts:** Vector fields, line/surface integrals, gradient, divergence, curl, Green's & Stokes' Theorems.
* **Implementation:** 3D vector field visualization and flux calculations using Python (`sympy`, `matplotlib`).

### 📊 Curve Fitting & Regression Analysis
* **Core Concepts:** Linear/polynomial regression, optimization methods, model complexity, dynamic data fitting, and overfitting constraints.
* **Tools:** `scipy.optimize`, `numpy`.

---

## 2. Astrophysics & Wave Mechanics

### 🪐 Kepler's Laws Verification
* **Project:** Validated Kepler's Third Law ($T^2 \propto a^3$) by applying custom curve-fitting algorithms (from Section 1) to astronomical datasets.
* **Tech Stack:** Python data pipeline using `pandas` and `scipy`.

### 🌊 Double-Slit Wave Interference
* **Project:** Simulated wave-particle interaction and phase interference patterns across customizable slit geometry.
* **Visualization:** Light intensity distribution curves generated via numerical wave equations.

---

## 3. Dynamical Systems & Epidemiology

### 🧮 Differential Equations & Partial Differential Equations (PDEs)
* **Core Concepts:** Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs) and spatio-temporal boundary problems governed by reaction-diffusion PDEs.
* **Motivation:** Developed mathematical frameworks required for continuous spatial modeling.

### 🦠 Spatio-Temporal Disease Simulation
* **Project:** Built a 2D spatial epidemic spread simulator based on continuous PDE discretization.
* **Output:** Real-time animated heatmaps showing disease diffusion dynamics over time.

---

## 4. Electrical Engineering & Signal Analysis

### ⚡ Circuit Theory & Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy (EIS)
* **Core Concepts:** AC circuit response, complex impedance, Nyquist/Bode plots, and equivalent circuit modeling.
* **Transition:** Analysis of non-linear circuit responses necessitated frequency-domain transformation tools.

### 🔄 Fourier Series & Fourier Transform
* **Core Concepts:** Continuous/Discrete Fourier Transforms, harmonic decomposition, and frequency spectrum analysis.
* **Application:** Deconstructed complex AC and EIS impedance signals into fundamental frequency components.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack
* **Language:** Python 3.x
* **Core Libraries:** `NumPy`, `SciPy`, `Matplotlib`, `SymPy`, `Pandas`
* **Documentation:** Jupyter Notebooks, Markdown, LaTeX
